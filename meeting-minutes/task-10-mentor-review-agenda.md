## 1. Walkthrough (≈20 min)

Nineteen slides in a fixed order, five of which are mandatory and cannot be cut
for time: **legal**, **identification**, **refusals**, **corrections**,
**quality**. If the clock runs short, the slides that go are from the other
fourteen.

The storyline: what was collected and under what permission → what the panel
control does to the monthly series → what is identified and what is not →
relative share, similarity, distinctiveness → what was refused and why → the
nine corrections → how the deck itself is checked.

## 2. The line the whole track sits on (≈10 min)

Three sentences a stakeholder would want, and why the repository will not say
any of them:

- *"Google's hiring grew 12% in 2023."* — four defensible panel treatments
  spread 99.05 index points around the 2023 level, so the sign is not
  identified.
- *"Google posts more jobs than Snowflake."* — a posting count is a count of the
  boards that syndicate a posting. Share of a fixed publisher pool is
  comparable; the level is not.
- *"Google pays more than Meta for research roles."* — Google discloses a salary
  in 4.02% of its 2023 postings. A median over that is a median over whoever
  chose to disclose.

Live, if asked: [`notebooks/02-the-refusal-boundary.ipynb`](../notebooks/02-the-refusal-boundary.ipynb)
runs the gates on any sentence the mentor wants to try.

## 4. Decisions to take away

- Whether the aligned team storyline uses this slot order for all four company
  tracks, or only the five mandatory slots are fixed and the rest are free.
- Whether the other tracks adopt the claim ledger, or write their decks by hand
  and accept that their numbers are not bound to a table.
- What the team does about the two structural limits that no amount of analysis
  fixes: one incomplete annual cycle, and a maximum useful forecast horizon of
  zero.

## 5. Points to raise against ourselves

- The wording gate is a floor, not a boundary: three of six tempting sentences
  clear it and are stopped at identification instead. A synonym walks past the
  regex; it does not walk past the ledger.
- C9 is the correction this task registered about itself — the deck is
  checkable, the delivery is not. Nothing in this repository tests whether the
  presentation was any good.
